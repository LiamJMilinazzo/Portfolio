Published Game

Nara Order

Nara Order is a single-player tycoon-style food stand game set in Nara Park and near Tōdai-ji, one of Japan’s most historically significant cultural locations. Players take on the role of a food stand employee serving a range of customers, from children ordering drinks and ramen to deer wanting biscuits and even Buddha requesting blessed water from the temple cleansing basin.

Genre: Tycoon / Time Management Mode: Single Player Setting: Nara, Japan

The gameplay revolves around reading the ticket shown at the top of the screen and quickly assembling the correct order before time runs out. Each successful order increases the player’s score, while missed orders reduce available lives. As the session continues, the pace intensifies, creating a simple but increasingly demanding gameplay loop built around speed, accuracy, and visual recognition.

What makes the project especially meaningful is how directly it reflects our group’s trip to Japan. The game’s setting is based on the archway we walked beneath before entering Tōdai-ji, and multiple items and characters are inspired by real details from Nara Park, including shika senbei, the temple water basin, deer interactions, architecture, and foods we personally experienced. The art direction was designed to capture a calm, semi-realistic anime atmosphere inspired by Japanese Buddhist temple environments and the accessible design philosophy often associated with Nintendo.

My contribution: I contributed to the creative development of the game’s visual and thematic direction, supported team collaboration, and helped shape a project centered on cultural respect, environmental inspiration, and accessible gameplay. The project also reflects skills developed through international collaboration and adapting ideas from firsthand historical and cultural experiences into interactive design.

Published Game

Sugar Scape

Sugar Scape is a survival-based arcade maze game created during my Japanese study abroad experience with an international team. Players move through a colorful maze using the arrow keys, hunting cute creatures to increase their score while trying to survive as the number of enemies steadily grows.

Genre: Survival / Arcade Format: Team Game Jam Project Controls: Arrow Keys + Space Bar

The core loop is fast and straightforward: roam the maze, stay mobile, and eat nearby animals with the space bar when they are within range. The more the player consumes, the more creatures appear, turning the game into a test of movement, timing, and score optimization. The goal is to survive as long as possible while pushing for a new high score.

The project’s playful aesthetic came from team-created artwork developed during the game jam. I worked alongside Jason, Yerik, Hizac, and other collaborators to help bring the game together. The final result reflects both the chaotic fun of arcade design and the collaborative energy of building something quickly with an international team.

My contribution: I was part of the game jam team that developed the project during study abroad, contributing to the collaborative design and development process while working across language and cultural differences. The project strengthened my ability to communicate ideas clearly, adapt in fast-paced creative settings, and help shape a game around a simple but addictive loop.
