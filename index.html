Featured Projects

Published games & collaborative work

Two published games built from real experiences — one rooted in a trip through Nara, one born from the chaos of an international game jam.

Tycoon / Time Management

Nara Order

A single-player tycoon-style food stand game set in Nara Park, near Tōdai-ji. You play as a food stand employee juggling orders from a rotating cast of customers — children, deer nudging you for biscuits, and even Buddha requesting blessed water from the temple cleansing basin.

Single Player Setting: Nara, Japan Published

The loop is focused: read the ticket, build the order fast, don't let time run out. Every detail traces back to something we actually saw in Nara — the archway before Tōdai-ji, the deer wandering the park, the shika senbei sold from carts along the path. The art aims for a calm, semi-realistic anime atmosphere influenced by Buddhist temple aesthetics and Nintendo's welcoming design philosophy.

My Role — Lead Developer

Wrote the full codebase and built all backend systems: customer logic, scoring, lives, pacing, and the complete interaction flow. Handled asset integration, connecting art and UI to gameplay for a clean final build.

Survival / Arcade

Sugar Scape

A survival arcade maze game built during my study abroad in Japan with an international team. You move through a colorful maze hunting cute creatures to rack up your score — but the more you eat, the more enemies appear. At some point, staying alive becomes the whole game.

International Game Jam Arrow Keys + Space Published

The core loop is tight: roam, stay mobile, eat nearby animals when they're in range. The enemy count scales as you play, so what starts as a score chase turns into a test of movement and timing. The result feels like exactly what it was — something fast, playful, and built by a group having fun with an absurd idea under a tight deadline.

My Role — Lead Developer

Designed the level layout, coded player and enemy movement, built time-based interactions, and added difficulty scaling. Also created artwork for the baby (player character) and the panda (main enemy).
